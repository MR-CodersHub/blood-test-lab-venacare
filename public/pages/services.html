Patient Clinical Preparation

Preparing for your at-home blood test

Follow these clinical guidelines to ensure maximum diagnostic precision and a smooth, gentle blood draw.

1. Hydrate with Water

Drink 2–3 glasses of plain water beforehand. Hydration expands plasma volume and makes veins visibly plumper and easier to access gently.

2. Observe Fasting Window

If your panel includes Glucose, CMP, or Lipids, fast for 10–12 hours. Early morning collection lets you sleep through most of the fast.

3. Medication Routine

Take your routine essential blood pressure medications with water. If taking thyroid medication, delay the dose until after the blood draw.

4. Relax in Comfort

Wear comfortable clothing with sleeves that roll up easily. Your phlebotomist will set up a clean, sterile workspace in your dining or living room.
