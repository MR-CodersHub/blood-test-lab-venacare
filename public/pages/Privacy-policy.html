Blood donation runs on trust. This policy explains exactly what VenaCare Blood Camp collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it and the controls you hold over it.

This Privacy Policy describes how VenaCare Blood Camp (“VenaCare”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data when you visit our website, book a camp, donate blood or respond to an emergency request. It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

1 · Information we collect

We collect only what a blood-donation network genuinely needs to operate:

  • Account data — name, email address, phone number, city and password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Donation data — blood group, donation dates, units given, certificates issued and deferral status.
  • Booking data — camps you reserve, attendance, organiser details for workplace drives.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser, device type and pages viewed, collected for security and reliability.
  • Communications — messages sent through the mediated inbox, support emails and survey responses.

2 · How we use your information

Your data is used to deliver the service you signed up for and nothing more incidental:

  1. Match you with compatible emergency requests near you, ranked by distance and eligibility.
  2. Run camps safely — registration, screening handover, certificates and post-donation follow-up.
  3. Send transactional messages: booking confirmations, day-50 reminders, request updates.
  4. Improve the platform using aggregated, anonymised usage statistics.
  5. Meet legal, safety and audit obligations required of a blood-donation intermediary.

We rely on consent (for alerts and marketing), contract performance (for bookings) and legitimate interest (for platform security) as our lawful bases.

3 · Health & screening data

Screening records are special-category data and receive protection far above ordinary account details. They are encrypted at rest, visible only to you and the supervising camp physician, and processed strictly for donor safety.

  • Never shared with employers, colleges, insurers or prospective employers.
  • Never used for advertising, profiling or eligibility decisions outside the camp.
  • Deleted 90 days after collection unless you actively opt into a long-term donor health profile.
  • Withdraw consent at any time from Settings → Health data, without affecting your account.

4 · Who we share with

We share the minimum necessary, with contractual safeguards, in these situations only:

  • Partner hospitals and blood banks — unit identity and custody records when a unit is handed over.
  • Cold-chain couriers — pickup details and temperature logs, never your full profile.
  • Regulators — statutory reporting where blood establishments are required to report by law.
  • Service providers — hosting, email delivery and analytics under written data-processing agreements.
We do not sell personal data. We do not rent it. We do not run third-party advertising trackers on donor accounts.

5 · Cookies & local storage

VenaCare uses a small number of cookies plus browser local storage. Local storage keeps your preferences on your own device — for example your theme choice (venacare-theme) and layout direction (venacare-rtl) — so the site looks the way you left it on your next visit.

  • Essential — session, security and load balancing. Always on.
  • Preferences — theme, language and layout direction, stored locally.
  • Analytics — aggregated page performance, disabled until you opt in where the law requires it.

Clear them at any time through your browser settings; the site remains fully usable without optional cookies.

6 · Data retention

We keep data only as long as it has a purpose:

  • Screening and vitals records — 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Donation history and certificates — for the life of your account, plus 24 months after closure.
  • Emergency request records — 12 months for audit and safety review, then anonymised.
  • Marketing consent — until you opt out; suppression lists are kept so we respect your choice.

7 · Security measures

Donor trust is the product. Our hosting runs on ISO 27001 certified infrastructure with encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, role-based access control, mandatory multi-factor authentication for staff, and annual third-party penetration testing.

  • Access to health data is logged and reviewed monthly.
  • Production access requires a hardware key, not a password alone.
  • Verified data breaches are reported to regulators within 72 hours and to affected users without undue delay.

8 · Your rights

Depending on your region, you can exercise any of the following free of charge:

  • Access — request a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate account or donation details.
  • Erasure — request deletion, subject to statutory blood-safety record keeping.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable JSON or CSV file.
  • Objection & opt-out — stop marketing communications and non-essential processing at any time.

We respond within 30 days. Identity verification is required before we release or delete records.

9 · Children's privacy

Donors must be 17 or older to create an account. Sixteen- and seventeen-year-olds may donate only with documented guardian consent, collected on paper at the camp rather than through the site. We never knowingly collect data from anyone under 16, and if we learn we have done so we delete it immediately.

10 · International transfers

Data is processed in the region where you live by default. Where a transfer is required — for example to a partner hospital in another country — we use standard contractual clauses, encryption in transit and a documented transfer-impact assessment before the data moves.

11 · Changes to this policy

Material changes are announced at least 14 days before they take effect, by email to account holders and a notice on the site. Minor clarifications are published here with a new “last updated” date. Previous versions remain available on request.

12 · Contact us

For any privacy question, complaint or rights request, contact our Data Protection Officer at help@venacare.org or write to 221 Meridian Ave, Central District. We aim to acknowledge every enquiry within one business hour and resolve it within 30 days.
