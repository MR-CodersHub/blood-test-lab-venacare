1 · Acceptance of terms

This agreement is between you and VenaCare Blood Camp. By creating an account, booking a camp, donating at a VenaCare site, or responding to an emergency request, you confirm that you are at least 17 years old (or 16 with documented guardian consent), that the information you provide is accurate, and that you accept these terms in full.

2 · Definitions

  • Platform — the VenaCare website, dashboards, APIs and messaging inbox.
  • Donor — a registered individual who offers to give blood.
  • Organiser — an office, campus or community group running a camp.
  • Institution — a hospital or blood bank using our matching infrastructure.
  • Camp — a scheduled donation event operated by VenaCare or a partner.

3 · The VenaCare service

VenaCare operates a blood-donation camp network and a donor-matching service. We facilitate contact between donors, organisers and institutions; we do not ourselves practise medicine, store blood for transfusion, or guarantee that a specific request will be filled. Emergency matching is provided free to individuals, funded by institutional plans.

4 · User accounts & responsibilities

You are responsible for the security of your credentials and for everything that happens under your account. You must keep your contact details and last-donation dates current, use one account per person, and notify us immediately at help@venacare.org if you suspect unauthorised access.

  • Do not share, sell or transfer your account.
  • Do not respond to requests on behalf of someone else without their consent.
  • Notify us if your phone number, email or eligibility status changes.

5 · Donor eligibility & medical disclaimer

Eligibility is decided by on-site screening — not by this platform. VenaCare provides information, not medical advice. Final acceptance rests with the supervising physician or nurse, who applies standard deferral rules for age, weight, haemoglobin, medication, recent travel, tattoos and illness.

  • General criteria: 17–65 years, at least 50 kg, valid photo ID, in good health.
  • Whole blood interval: 56 days. Platelets: 14 days. Deferrals may be temporary or permanent.
  • If you are deferred, follow the guidance given at the camp; a deferral is not a diagnosis.
  • Never donate while unwell — call the hotline and we will reschedule you free of charge.

6 · Organiser commitments

Organisers using the Camp Organizer plan or booking a drive agree to provide a safe, accessible venue of at least 800 sq ft with power access, promote the drive honestly, and secure any internal permissions required. VenaCare obtains the public health permits, insurance and biomedical-waste handling for the event.

  • Confirm headcount and target numbers at least seven days before the drive.
  • Do not advertise the camp with claims we have not approved in writing.
  • Allow our crew access from one hour before until one hour after the scheduled window.

7 · Fees, billing & trial

The Donor plan is free forever. Paid plans are billed in advance, monthly or annually, and renew automatically until cancelled. Every paid plan starts with a 14-day trial that requires no card; if you do nothing, it downgrades to the free plan automatically.

  • Upgrades take effect immediately and are pro-rated for the remainder of the cycle.
  • Downgrades take effect at the next billing date, with no loss of paid time.
  • Registered NGOs and university clubs receive 40% off any plan on proof of registration.
  • Invoicing and bank transfer are available for Hospital accounts; all major cards accepted elsewhere.
  • Refunds are pro-rata within 14 days of a first payment; after that, access continues to period end.
