Donating Blood

Eligibility, timing, safety and what to expect at the camp.

Donors must be 17–65 years old, weigh at least 50 kg, be in good health, and hold a valid photo ID. Temporary deferrals apply for recent illness, travel, tattoos or medication — the on-site nurse makes the final call.
Whole blood every 56 days. Platelets every 14 days, up to 24 times a year. Plasma follows a 28-day interval. Your dashboard tracks the clock for each track automatically.
There is a brief pinch when the needle goes in — most donors rate it 2 out of 10. The draw itself is painless; some donors feel mild pressure as the vein is accessed.
Every needle is sterile, single-use and opened in front of you. There is no risk of contracting any infection through donation. The bigger risk is leaving shelves empty.

Accounts & Billing

Accounts, privacy and managing paid plans.

No — walk-ins are welcome at every camp. An account only adds certificate storage, history, booking reminders and donor search.
Screening data is encrypted at rest, stored on ISO 27001 certified infrastructure, and never shared with employers, colleges or insurers. You can delete your profile at any time.
Settings → Billing → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the paid period, then drop to the free Donor plan. No cancellation fees.

Safety & Privacy

Testing, transfusion notifications and keeping your details private.

Every unit is screened for HIV, Hepatitis B & C, malaria, syphilis and blood group before release. Units that fail screening are destroyed under medical supervision.
Yes — opt into transfusion alerts and you will receive an anonymous SMS when your unit reaches a patient, when the hospital releases that status.
